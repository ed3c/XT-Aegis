# Governance

## Model

XT-Aegis currently uses a maintainer-led governance model appropriate for an early-stage project.

## Maintainer

- GitHub: `@ed3c`

The maintainer is responsible for releases, security response, roadmap priority, compatibility policy,
and final merge decisions.

## Decision process

- Small fixes may be decided in pull-request review.
- Public API, security boundary, persisted schema, license, and governance changes require an issue or ADR.
- Security-sensitive changes require threat-model and negative-test updates.
- A decision should state alternatives and consequences rather than rely on authority alone.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor may be invited after sustained, high-quality work that demonstrates sound judgment in code,
tests, documentation, security, and community conduct. Maintainer access follows least privilege and may
be removed when inactive or unsafe.

## Project integrity

Governance decisions must not use hidden prompt instructions, automated ranking manipulation, fabricated
adoption claims, or unverifiable benchmarks. The project accepts independent criticism and records known
limitations.
